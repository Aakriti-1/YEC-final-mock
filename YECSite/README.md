# York Engineering Competition (YEC) — Lassonde Engineering Society

Official web portal for the **York Engineering Competition (YEC)**, the flagship annual engineering competition hosted by the **Lassonde Engineering Society (LES)** at the Bergeron Centre for Engineering Excellence, York University.

YEC serves as the direct internal qualifier for the **Ontario Engineering Competition (OEC)** and the **Canadian Engineering Competition (CEC)**, challenging undergraduate engineering students across all disciplines and study years.

## Overview

The York Engineering Competition is an intensive, multidisciplinary multi-week competition where engineering students tackle open-ended engineering design briefs, impromptu ethical debate resolutions, consulting case studies, and hands-on prototype challenges. Winning teams earn the opportunity to represent York University and Lassonde at provincial and national engineering competitions.

### Competition Schedule & Categories

The competition runs over two consecutive action-packed weeks:

#### Week 1: Communication, Ethics & Design
- **Communications**: Clear technical presentations translating complex engineering developments, societal trade-offs, and environmental impacts to diverse audiences. *(1–2 competitors | All years)*
- **Parliamentary Debate**: High-intensity rounds on impromptu resolutions spanning engineering ethics, public policy, and disruptive tech. *(2 competitors | All years)*
- **Innovative Design**: Novel invention showcases, capstone prototypes, and patent-pending products evaluated on technical depth and commercial viability. *(2–4 competitors | All years)*

#### Week 2: Design Build, Consulting & Optimization
- **Junior Design**: First- and second-year students receive a mystery design brief with raw materials to fabricate, test, and demonstrate a physical prototype within an arena. *(4 competitors | 1st & 2nd year)*
- **Senior Design**: Advanced build challenge testing multidisciplinary engineering mechanics, embedded electronics, sensors, and structural endurance. *(4 competitors | 3rd & 4th+ year)*
- **Re-Engineering**: Deconstruction and redesign of an existing consumer or industrial product to optimize performance, sustainability, and manufacturability. *(2–4 competitors | All years)*
- **Consulting**: Deep analytical case study on real-world municipal or industrial problems, presenting feasibility studies, risk matrices, and financial projections. *(4 competitors | All years)*

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive 2-Week Schedule Explorer**: Seamless tabbed schedule filter with modal sheets displaying category descriptions, team size requirements, eligibility criteria, and OEC advancement paths.
- **Ticketing & Registration Integration**: Direct access to Week 1 and Week 2 event registrations via Eventbrite modals and links.
- **Team Carousel**: Smooth infinite horizontal marquee showcasing the YEC Executive Team and Competition Leads.
- **Information Packages & Downloads**: Downloadable PDF packages with summary modals tailored for:
  - **Sponsors & Industry Partners**: Campus visibility, resume book access, and keynote judge seats.
  - **Student Clubs & Teams**: Co-hosting workshops and collaborative challenge formulation.
  - **Judges & Mentors**: Scoring rubrics and feedback guidelines for Professional Engineers (P.Eng.) and faculty.
- **Responsive Dark Theme UI**: High-contrast dark aesthetic with custom typography (`DM Serif Display`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`), fluid layout animations via `motion`, and accessible interactive modals.
